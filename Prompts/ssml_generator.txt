Task: Convert narration text into SSML suitable for English TTS.

Inputs:
- scene_id (int)
- narration (string)
- estimated_seconds (int)

Rules:
- Insert <break time="...ms"/> at natural sentence boundaries: for sentences estimated ≤8s use 300ms, for 8–16s use 500ms.
- Add prosody/emphasis for numeric facts: wrap numbers with <emphasis level="moderate">...</emphasis>.
- Ensure SSML is valid and minimal (no extraneous commentary).

Output JSON: {"scene_id":int, "ssml":string, "timings_hint": [seconds_per_sentence,...]}
